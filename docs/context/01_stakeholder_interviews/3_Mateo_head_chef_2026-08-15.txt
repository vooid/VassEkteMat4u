STAKEHOLDER INTERVIEW 3 - Head chef
Interviewee: Mateo Rinaldi (head chef)
Date: 15 Aug 2026   Duration: 30 min
[SYNTHETIC DATA - created for INFO212 Lab 7. All persons and companies are fictional.]

Q: What do you need from the system?
A: One screen in the kitchen. Tablet on the wall. For each day: which orders, how many portions of each
dish, allergies in red. I do not want to log in with long passwords with flour on my hands - a PIN
is fine.

Q: Anything about menus?
A: Menus change with the season, four times a year. I want to edit the menu myself, and ingredients
should be linked to suppliers, so we can show origin like Ingrid wants. And I need a prep list:
total kilos of each ingredient for the next three days.

Q: What happens when the internet is down?
A: It happened twice last winter. The kitchen must still see today's orders. Printing works too,
but the screen should not just go blank.

Q: Anything else?
A: Please do not make it slow. The old booking plugin on the website took ten seconds to load a page.
