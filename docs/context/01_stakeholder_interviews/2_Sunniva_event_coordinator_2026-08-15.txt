STAKEHOLDER INTERVIEW 2 - Event coordinator
Interviewee: Sunniva Berg (event coordinator, Ekte Mat AS)
Date: 15 Aug 2026   Duration: 50 min
[SYNTHETIC DATA - created for INFO212 Lab 7. All persons and companies are fictional.]

Q: Walk us through what happens when an inquiry arrives.
A: I read the e-mail, check the calendar in Google to see if we have capacity that day, reply with
a quote in Word, and when they say yes I add a row in "Bestillinger 2025.xlsx". Then I print the
order and put it on the kitchen board. Changes in guest numbers come by phone, and I have to
remember to update both the sheet and the printout. Sometimes I forget.

Q: How long does it take to answer a request?
A: On a normal week one or two days. In December and in May it can be a week. People get annoyed.

Q: What about the deposit Ingrid mentioned?
A: Honestly, corporate clients hate prepayment. Fjordtek and the other big firms want one invoice per
month with all their lunches on it, sent as EHF. If we force a deposit on them, we will lose them.
For weddings a deposit makes sense.

Q: What else would help you?
A: A calendar that shows how many guests we already have per day, so I do not overbook. Our kitchen
can do about 400 covers a day, 250 if there is a wedding with a served dinner. And automatic
reminders to customers about final guest numbers seven days before the event.

Q: Allergies?
A: Very important. Every order has to show allergies and diets per guest group, and the kitchen must
confirm they have seen it. We had a nut incident in 2024 - nobody was hurt, but it was close.
