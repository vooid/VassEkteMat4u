STAKEHOLDER INTERVIEW 1 - Owner / daglig leder
Interviewee: Ingrid Solheim (founder and owner, Ekte Mat AS)
Interviewer: project team   Date: 14 Aug 2026   Duration: 45 min
[SYNTHETIC DATA - created for INFO212 Lab 7. All persons and companies are fictional.]

Q: What is the main problem today?
A: Everything comes in through e-mail and the phone. My coordinator copies it into an Excel sheet.
We lose inquiries. Last December we found three julebord requests in the spam folder after the date
had passed. That is maybe 60 000 kroner we never even saw.

Q: What should the new system do first?
A: Customers must be able to see our menus and prices and send a booking request themselves,
at any hour. I want a proper order overview for the kitchen, and I want the system to take a
30 % deposit when someone books - no deposit, no booking. We have had too many no-shows at weddings.

Q: Payment?
A: Vipps for private customers, definitely. Companies want an invoice. We use Fiken for accounting
today and I do not want to change that.

Q: Anything about the brand?
A: Authentic, seasonal, honest about where the food comes from. I would love every menu to show
where the lamb or the fish comes from - the farm, the boat. That is our story.

Q: Budget and timeline?
A: We have about 450 000 NOK for the first version including one year of hosting. I want something
live before the 2027 wedding season, so by 1 March 2027 at the latest.

Q: Who else should we talk to?
A: Sunniva, who coordinates events, and Mateo in the kitchen. And maybe Fjordtek - they are our
biggest corporate customer.
