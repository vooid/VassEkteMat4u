# As-is process: handling a catering order (described by Sunniva, 15 Aug 2026)
[SYNTHETIC DATA - INFO212 Lab 7]

1. Customer sends an e-mail or calls (phone calls are noted on paper).
2. Coordinator reads the request, usually within 1-2 working days (up to 7 days in peak season).
3. Coordinator checks the Google Calendar for capacity. If the day is "full" (gut feeling), she proposes another date.
4. Coordinator writes a quote in Word from a template and e-mails it as PDF.
5. If the customer accepts by e-mail, the coordinator adds a row to the Excel sheet "Bestillinger".
6. For weddings, the coordinator sends a Vipps payment request for a deposit (sometimes forgotten).
7. Coordinator prints the order and pins it on the kitchen board.
8. Changes (guest count, allergies) arrive by phone/e-mail; coordinator updates Excel and re-prints (sometimes forgotten).
9. 7 days before the event the coordinator phones the customer to confirm final numbers (if she has time).
10. Kitchen prepares, driver delivers. Driver gets the address from the printout.
11. After the event the coordinator creates an invoice in Fiken manually, or a Vipps request for private customers.
12. Once a month the owner reconciles Fiken with the Excel sheet.
